      BEHIND THE NUMBERS

      Transparent by design.

      Four published ED metrics form the pressure index. Higher waiting times and did-not-wait rates, and lower timely-care and four-hour-stay rates, contribute more pressure.

      Median waiting time
      30%
      Seen within recommended time
      30%
      Patients who did not wait
      20%
      ED stay within 4 hours
      20%

      Values are normalized comparatively across facilities. Missing metrics are handled by renormalizing available weights, not by treating missing measurements as zero. Facilities with no available metrics have an unknown score.

      Population and ED analytics are separate analytical streams because the ED source does not provide an LGA field. No hospital-to-LGA mapping is inferred.

      Explore with context. This index is an exploratory heuristic and is not clinically validated. It should not be used for clinical or operational decision-making without further validation.
